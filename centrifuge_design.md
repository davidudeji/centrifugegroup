CENTRIFUGE GROUP — MASTER WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION

ROLE
You are a senior product designer, brand strategist, UX architect, and frontend engineer.

Design and build a premium, modern corporate technology website for:

CENTRIFUGE GROUP

The website must feel like a serious technology company that builds and operates enterprise software, healthcare technology, logistics platforms, data systems, infrastructure, and digital products.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT copy any website, layout, illustration, wording, branding, or visual asset from another company.

Use the following design references only as inspiration for specific design principles:

- OpenAI → simplicity, editorial hierarchy, whitespace
- Linear → product-led storytelling, dark product sections, software UI presentation
- Apple → premium product presentation, photography, whitespace
- Notion → clear information hierarchy and approachable product storytelling
- Shopify → ecommerce/store UX
- Modern enterprise technology companies → credibility, precision, clarity

The final design must feel like an ORIGINAL Centrifuge website.

==================================================
63. IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT RULE
==================================================

Do not build everything as one giant component.

Use:

Feature-based architecture
Reusable components
Strong TypeScript types
Service abstraction
Reusable layouts
Reusable forms
Reusable tables
Reusable cards

Keep public website, storefront, and admin dashboard logically separated.

==================================================
64. DATA RULE
==================================================

Use deterministic demo data for development.

Create realistic demo:

Products
Categories
Customers
Orders
Inventory
Analytics
Articles
Jobs

But clearly separate demo data from real production data.

NEVER invent:

Client names
Revenue
Awards
Certifications
Testimonials
Business results
Employee numbers
Customer numbers
Product specifications

unless they are provided or verified.

==================================================
65. SUCCESS CRITERIA
==================================================

The final result should pass this test:

If someone visits the homepage for 10 seconds, they should understand:

1. What Centrifuge is.
2. What it builds.
3. Who it serves.
4. What products it offers.
5. Why its technology is credible.
6. How to contact the company.

If someone wants to buy technology hardware:

They should be able to find the store quickly.

If a customer wants to buy:

Product
→ Cart
→ Checkout
→ Confirmation

If an administrator wants to manage the store:

Login
→ Dashboard
→ Products
→ Inventory
→ Orders
→ Customers
→ Analytics

Everything should feel like ONE coherent Centrifuge ecosystem.
